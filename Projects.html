Draw The Night Sky

Unity C# Project made in cooperation with the U.S. Corp of Marines Made as my senior project! It's an educational game that tries to teach the player astrology and gives them the tools to find the constellations with tools they can find at home. This is possible due to Yale's Bright Star Catalogue providing accurate astronomical mapping.
